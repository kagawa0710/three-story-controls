Three Story Controls

A Three.js camera toolkit for creating interactive 3D stories with flexible camera rigs and control schemes.

Maintained by gawatech

FreeMovement Controls

First-person controls to move freely around the scene using keyboard, mouse, or touch.

Interactive

Scroll + 3DOF Controls

Scroll through the page to scrub through a camera animation with subtle mouse-driven rotation.

Scroll-based

StoryPoint + 3DOF Controls

Transition between specific points in the scene with smooth camera movements.

Story-driven

PathPoint Controls

Transition between specific frames of a pre-defined camera animation path.

Path-based

Camera Helper

Visual tool for authoring camera animations and points of interest. Export to JSON for use in control schemes.

Authoring Tool
