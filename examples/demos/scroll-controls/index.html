Scroll Controls Demo

This demo showcases the ScrollControls component, which allows you to scrub through a camera animation by scrolling through the page. The camera smoothly follows a predefined path as you scroll.

As you scroll down, the 3D scene will animate, and captions will appear at key moments in the story. This technique is commonly used in interactive journalism and immersive web experiences.

The camera begins its journey, smoothly transitioning as you continue to scroll through the experience.

Each scroll position maps to a specific point along the camera's animation path, creating a seamless connection between navigation and storytelling.

The 3DOF (three degrees of freedom) controls add subtle mouse-driven rotation, making the experience feel more responsive and interactive.

This pattern is ideal for guided narratives where you want to control the pace of the story while allowing exploration within each scene.

The ScrollControls component works by mapping the scroll position of a DOM element to a specific frame of a Three.js AnimationClip. Combined with the 3DOF controls, it creates an engaging hybrid of passive storytelling and active exploration.

This approach is particularly effective for long-form content where you want to guide users through a narrative while maintaining their engagement through subtle interactivity.

The camera path and points were created using the Camera Helper tool, which exports JSON data that can be directly used by ScrollControls, PathPointControls, and StoryPointControls.
